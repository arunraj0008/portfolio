methodology walkthrough — illustrative example

Phishing Email Triage — Analyst Methodology

Headers → Sender → Content → Links → Verdict

A worked example of the triage process I follow on suspected phishing emails, using generic, illustrative indicators rather than one specific real inbox case.

  • HEADERSCheck SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment and the originating IP's reverse DNS and geolocation against the sender's claimed identity.
  • SENDERCompare the sending domain's registration date, registrar, and privacy settings against the brand it claims to represent — a domain registered days ago is a strong lookalike signal.
  • CONTENTFlag urgency language, generic greetings, spelling inconsistencies, and authority impersonation as compounding indicators rather than single points of failure.
  • LINKSResolve the actual destination behind any shortened or display text link, inspect certificate issue date, and check whether the landing page mimics a known login flow.
